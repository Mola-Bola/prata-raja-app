## Feel reference (baseline shipped in 0.2.2; remaining polish in BACKLOG)

Preserve the owner list in [BACKLOG](BACKLOG.md) and the newer [0.2.0 clip findings](research/owner-refs-2026-10-13.md). Historical target list; do not treat every detail as delivered:

1. **Danger first:** brighter/thicker boss lanes; clear counter hit flash and reacting HP pill; shorter boss approach. Place the boss bar away from the wave/level strip.
2. **Fix confusing rewards/UI:** correct chest pity copy/counts and verify each open advances them once; remove Start-button ghosting under sheets; clear landed breads that have no gameplay role.
3. **Hits and throws:** bigger outlined bouncing damage numbers, crit tier, merged DoT ticks; enemy flash/squash/knockback reaction. Animate Kannan's anticipation → hand release → follow-through with rendered poses; breads spin, trail, shrink in perspective and puff at release.
4. **Counter attacks:** per-pest lunge/bite/slam, steel impact, restrained shake, hurt cue and HP response. The attack must read as the cause of damage.
5. **Street staging:** larger far pests/numbers, flatter perspective (0.42 far scale is a candidate, not a lock), far-end/drain entry with a short entrance and curved wandering. Fight nearer visible mid-street; no straight spawn rows or building/wall emergence. Re-check balance.
6. **Pacing:** fill the roughly 15-second late-wave empty gaps; shorten boss idling without removing the dodge/throw recovery windows. Measure active pressure and clear time.
7. **Counter life:** bowls and bread stacks in fixed perspective slots with contact shadows/tray/basket; subtle rendered tawa steam. Owned bowls/breads must read as the build.
8. **Cards and effects:** repeat damping/wider legal pool, meaningful before/after readout; paused dim arena with short staggered card reveal. Shape-labelled aura at true radius, brief thin chain arcs and the existing danger band. Keep new card copy short.
9. **Theme:** banana-leaf/steel-thali/brass-kolam/rangoli borders. Dip flavour review after S3 integration; separate cosmetic renaming from mechanic changes so recipes remain compatible.

Optional Tawa Heat and Clean Flip remain candidates after the baseline feels good. Add one rhythm mechanic and test it; do not combine the earlier 0.30-second Clean Flip and 0.60-second windup proposals into two meters. Show heat on the tawa, with a bounded payoff and recovery windows.

## Why this order holds

Links/ownership precede multi-food triggers. Persistent choices precede card bias. Charge/ward accounting precedes drink and aura uniques. Crafting follows meaningful affixes. New bosses test established builds before the game asks for more permanent stats.
S3 shipped in 0.3.0; preserve its recipe pools, evolution eligibility and chapter gates during rebuilds. Prototype a new engine in the deterministic core before commissioning its full rendered effects.
At each phase, show one next goal on wake-up: “one ingredient to Murtabak”, “one boss attempt to the night”, or “try a pepper recipe”. Offer up to two optional alternatives; no panel of chores and no clock telling a child they have fallen behind.
Training's current roughly 10–15-run finish is a milestone. Recipes and alternative signatures supply the next goal; do not extend it with an uncapped damage sink. Coins and ingredients remain the economy, with free respec and free repeat attempts.
Later chapter places follow the owner's real-Singapore direction; confirm the order/fictionalised presentation before art production. No real stall names or logos. The Grade Inspector spirit and other hawker heroes stay later candidates, not required 1.0 scope.

## Verification and shipping

Builders run `bash scripts/check.sh` before reporting done (`SKIP_DB=1` only when the local bench is unavailable), plus focused web assertions for the changed flow. No cloud tests or production data work. Use the existing bot for changed combat and real phones for feel/performance; screenshots only for changed screens.
The controller reviews, merges and ships through `app-ship`. Research workers commit on their branch; no direct deploy. Give each implementation slice its contract, migration, bounded content and exit evidence before another slice starts.
Money remains late: the proposed gate requires two four-week outside-family cohorts, adequate D7 return, second-build use, non-payer interviews and support demand. If it fails, keep improving play; do not insert energy, scarcity or paid stats to manufacture traction.
