# Prata Raja v3: rough cut-out, darker ascension

Owner direction, 15 Oct (late). Builds on docs/plans/v2-concept.md (systems: statuses, combos, gear sets, mythics, builds) and keeps it as reference. v3 changes tone, material, setting and the height of the progression. Art: ~/prata-raja-assets/v3/ (Codex batch 5, refs in v3/refs/).

## What changes from v2
- **Tone:** starts humble and warm, then climbs into real awe. Lv 1 is a tired hawker at dawn; Lv 100 is a legend of the night street. Humour comes from situations, not baby faces.
- **Material:** rough cut-out. Thick card and bark layers with torn edges, woodcut-style carving, deep furrowed bark texture, weathering. Not clean, not toy-like. Grounded adult proportions.
- **Setting:** Singapore nostalgia, 1960s–80s: shophouse five-foot-ways under sodium lamps, HDB blocks and void decks, kopitiam, pasar malam, ice-ball and kacang puteh carts, trishaws, orange phone booths, monsoon rain.
- **Palette:** weathered sepia, charcoal and faded shophouse pastels; power glows in ember orange, molten gold, curry-dark red and flour white against the dark.

## Ascension: five tiers per path
| Path | Lv 10 | Lv 30 | Lv 60 | Lv 100 (legend) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crisp | Flipper | Crispmaster | Golden Edge | **Thousand-Layer Raja**: a storm of razor prata layers, molten-gold edges |
| Soak | Gravy Hand | Monsoon Cook | Banjir Raja | **Monsoon Leviathan**: rises out of a curry flood under a storm |
| Heat | Chilli Boy | Wok Blazer | Sambal Raja | **Ember Wrath**: charred-bark skin cracked with sambal fire, smoke and embers |
| Dough | Dough Slinger | Stretch Artist | Murtabak Titan | **Dough Colossus**: giant, binding pests in dough strands inside a flour storm |
Each tier is visibly bigger: posture, prop scale, armour from hawker materials (tawa iron, burnt ghee, steel katori, dough, chillies, curry), aura. The Lv 100 form is a run-long transformation earned by a meter (e.g. fill it with combos, then hold Dodge to ascend for 20 s).

## Home and streets
- **Home:** Kannan's stall at an HDB void deck at night (stools, letterboxes, phone booth, kopi uncle); it grows as bonds with regulars grow.
- **Streets:** shophouse five-foot-way (Kopitiam Street), old hawker centre in the monsoon, pasar malam, wet market at 4 a.m., food street. Each has its own pests and boss (King Cockroach, Rat King, a moth swarm queen).
- **Bookends:** each run ends at dawn back at the real stall, steam rising, regulars arriving: warm and a little melancholic. The daydream fades; the tips are real.

## Relics (mythics, darker)
Same rules as v2's twelve mythics; they are now storied relics: an ancient cracked black-iron tawa with molten gold in the cracks, a scorched towel trailing afterimages, Ah Ma's worn brass ladle dripping curry like sap, a smoke-blackened seven-chilli jar, a thorned durian-husk crown.

## Unchanged from v2
Statuses (Dough, Wet, Burn, Crisp) and the six reactions; gear slots, sets and stacking; the Kitchen as one build screen; throwing feel (flip throw, catch-back, dodge spray, Sizzler); crunch and sound pillars; the operating model in v2-spec.
