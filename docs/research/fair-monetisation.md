# Fair monetisation, if players want the game to grow

13 Oct 2026. Owner rule: no paid power, no dollar-gated progression or caps. Today the game has no ads or purchases. This is a later decision, not a shop specification to build now.
This extends the retention cautions in [engagement](engagement.md) and the existing [Archero](teardown-archero.md) / [Habby](teardown-habby.md) research. Sources below are public wikis and guides, accessed today. Indexed excerpts were used where wiki access was blocked. Proposed rules are our judgement, not claims that these games are perfectly fair.

## Precedents, with the catch made explicit

| Game / model | What earns money | Useful lesson / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| PoE | Cosmetics, extra stash and account utility. Premium tabs also provide easier public trade listing. [Microtransactions](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Microtransaction), [stash](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Stash). | Sell expression. Stash convenience can become an effective cost of serious collecting; our free storage must support every viable build, with no paid storage ceiling. |
| Warframe | Platinum buys cosmetics and slots, but also equipment, upgrades, boosters and crafting rushes. Some Platinum is player-tradable. [Platinum](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Platinum), [trading](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Trading). | Broad earnability and visible purchases are useful; it is **not** a cosmetic-only precedent. Copy expression and generous access, not bought gear or faster power progression. |
| Deep Rock Galactic | A paid base game plus cosmetic/supporter DLC. DLC does not affect gameplay. Its Performance Pass is free; previous passes remain selectable. [DLC](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Downloadable_Content), [pass](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Maintenance). | Supporters can fund shared updates. A missed season need not erase rewards. Our paid lane can stay cosmetic and remain available after its season. |
| Brotato | A premium version exists alongside a separate mobile version with ads/IAP. [Mobile tips guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/brotato/tips/). | “Buy the game” is a clear offer. Name the version: the free mobile economy is not evidence that the premium one sells power. |
| Vampire Survivors | Low-cost premium releases, free and paid expansions; mobile uses optional ads. [Public overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors). | Modest upfront value can work. Paid food/weapon expansions and ad-powered revives would conflict with our stronger rule; borrow the price clarity, not those power differences. |
| Hades | The original PC/console game is sold as a complete game; run and permanent upgrades are earned inside it. [Public overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_%28video_game%29). | Depth, story and replay value can be the product. This is the original PC/console precedent, not a claim about every mobile distribution deal. |

## The Archero / Habby anti-goal

Archero's currency guide ties bought gems to gear chests, revives and energy. Entry stops when energy runs out; its author calls that a drawback. This is documented friction, not a claim about all players. [Archero currency/energy guide, 2020](https://progameguides.com/archero/archero-gems-coins-energy-guide/).
Archero 2's guide recommends premium supply/ad-free cards, power chests and daily mode chores. Our criticism is the loop they create: improvement becomes shopping or exhausting daily allowances. The game's enjoyable combat does not require us to adopt that economy. [Archero 2 beginner guide, 2025](https://progameguides.com/archero-2/archero-2-beginners-guide/).
Avoid energy, paid rerolls/revives, power chests, bought ingredients, XP/coin boosts, bought crafting attempts, paid heroes with stats, paid higher rarity, and spending-based matchmaking. Chests earned through play remain free; never sell their keys or odds.

## Offers that can earn without power

| Offer | Player buys | Free player's guarantee |
|---|---|---|
| Stall decorations / outfits | Lanterns, signboards, towels, crowns and wake-up poses. Preview the full item at a clear price. | Same hitbox, visibility, damage and mechanics. A basic appearance for each progression milestone still comes from play. |
| Supporter pack / optional “buy the game” | One purchase: an outfit, soundtrack/art extras and a thank-you plaque. | Existing chapters and all future power systems remain accessible. Buying never raises a stat or unlock cap. |
| Supporter pass | Paid rewards are cosmetic only. Free track can offer normal play rewards on equal terms. | All mechanics and equal progression remain available. Archive purchased tracks; no expiry, streak penalty or catch-up fee. |
| Loadout convenience | Extra named presets or folders; save manual setup time. | Generous free presets, free respec and free import/export. Every build and all equipment remain usable; never sell equipped slots. |
| Stash convenience | Better organisation or more immediately visible display pages. | Expandable free storage with no dollar-gated total cap; no forced sale or loss of uniques. Paid organisation cannot increase resources earned. |
| Fewer ads / one-time ad removal | Only if a later optional cosmetic-ad model earns its place. Current game stays ad-free. | No forced interruptions, power rewards, better odds or revives from ads. Non-watchers can earn cosmetic rewards through normal play too. |

Do not launch every offer together. Start with one supporter pack; learn whether anyone wants to support the stall. Cosmetics need to look good in the hub, where effects cannot obscure a run. Buy with visible local-currency prices; avoid a gem balance that hides cost.
Convenience must remove clicks, not a deliberately created shortage. If free storage, presets or controls feel cramped, fix the baseline before selling anything. No paid gameplay DLC under this owner direction; a cosmetic theme can accompany a chapter released to everyone.

## Traction gate and fairness checks

Proposed gate, not an industry benchmark: two consecutive four-week cohorts of at least 100 outside-family new players; D7 return at least 15% in each; at least 20% of returning players try a second build family. Add five interviews with non-payers and evidence of repeated requests to support the game. Small family cohorts cannot establish this gate.
Proceed only when core readability, save stability and the build loop pass, the owner reviews the evidence, and a support-cost estimate shows the small offer can fund upkeep. Revenue is not proof that the fun or fairness passed.
Measure cosmetic interest, refund reasons, retention and time to unlock by payer/non-payer cohorts; investigate differences rather than assuming causation. Do not tune enemies, offers or drops against spending. No targeting children or using a death, loss or near-miss as a sales prompt.
Before release: identical loadout + seed + inputs must produce identical combat and power rewards across purchase states. Every power item has a published ordinary-play acquisition path. Save migrations preserve access. Offline play survives entitlement/network failure; paid cosmetics can restore later.
Keep the fairness promise on the shop screen in one sentence: “Support the stall. Every build is earned by playing.” This is product copy proposed for a future release; implementation still routes names through the brand files.
