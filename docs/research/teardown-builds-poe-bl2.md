# Build engines: Path of Exile, Borderlands 2 and MapleStory

Research date: 13 Oct 2026. This extends [the first Maple/PoE teardown](teardown-maple-poe.md), [VS/Brotato](teardown-vs-brotato-oss.md) and [engagement](engagement.md). It studies behaviour, not balance values or the current strongest build.
Scope: **PoE 1**, BL2, and modern MapleStory; region and patch differences matter. Named combinations are examples, not required shopping lists. All Prata Raja adaptations below are design judgements.
Sources are public wiki articles and readable guides. No client files, datamined databases, PoEDB, RePoE, save editors or extracted item tables were used. Wiki search excerpts were used when direct access returned 403; this is marked in the source notes. No source numbers are imported into our balance.

## Archetype patterns

| Archetype | Mechanical engine and scaling axes | Example pieces that make it work | Cost / lesson for the stall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Righteous Fire | A burning aura damages nearby enemies and burns its owner. Scale life/energy shield, fire DoT and area; regeneration and maximum fire resistance sustain it. It is not an on-hit engine. | RF + Burning Damage + Elemental Focus; Increased Area or Concentrated Effect chooses coverage versus focus. Purity of Fire, Rise of the Phoenix and Ruby Flask help sustain; Chieftain or Inquisitor supplies a defensive route. [Mechanics](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Righteous_Fire), [example links](https://www.poe-vault.com/guides/righteous-fire-inquisitor-build-guide). | Recovery becomes an offensive investment. A tawa aura can drain HP; low recovery and distant shooters remain weaknesses. Never treat aura ticks as repeated hits. |
| Essence Drain / Contagion | ED applies a chaos DoT; Contagion spreads both debuffs when a jointly affected enemy dies, carrying remaining duration. Scale DoT, duration and debuff coverage rather than repeated hit damage. | ED + Contagion; Efficacy / Void Manipulation suit the DoT route; Despair and Wither amplify chaos damage taken. Occultist offers chaos specialisation; Trickster offers sustain. [ED mechanics](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Essence_Drain), [historical Trickster links](https://www.poe-vault.com/guides/essence-drain-trickster-passive-tree-gem-links). | One bread marks, another supplies spread. Kill order and pack density matter; spreading does not refresh duration forever. A lone boss needs sustained application, not ever more ED stacks. |
| Toxic Rain | Arrows create temporary pods; overlapping pod DoTs stack. The initial arrow, pod DoT and final burst are distinct damage components. Area changes both coverage and overlap. | Toxic Rain; duration, attack rate and chaos DoT investment; Pathfinder flask sustain. Caustic Arrow + Manaforged Arrows is one secondary-clear route. [Mechanics](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Toxic_Rain), [Pathfinder guide](https://www.poe-vault.com/guides/toxic-rain-pathfinder-build-guide). | Lingering food patches reward holding pests in place. More projectiles or area does not automatically mean more boss damage. Show overlap; avoid hidden radius breakpoints. |
| Poison | Eligible hits apply independently expiring chaos DoTs. Many fast hits build many stacks; hit damage and poison have separate scaling rules. Toxic Rain's pod DoT is not itself poison. | Chance to Poison / poison chance, duration and chaos/ailment scaling; Pathfinder's Master Toxicist can spread poison during flask effects. [Poison](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Poison), [Pathfinder](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Pathfinder). | Idli can build spice stacks. Pay for ramp time and keep a finite stack cap. Do not scale each tick from already amplified hit damage. |
| Ignite | A strong eligible hit establishes a burn; by default the strongest ignite deals damage, rather than every application stacking like poison. Spread turns a large first hit into clear. | Elementalist Shaper of Flames allows all damage to ignite. Burning Damage, Deadly Ailments and Ignite Proliferation express different support choices; Berek's Respite spreads an equivalent ignite on kill. [Ignite](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Ignite), [Shaper](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Shaper_of_Flames), [Berek](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Berek%27s_Respite). | Loaded murtabak makes one big scorch. Fast weak shots should not replace it with a worse burn. Spread copies remaining life, with bounded range. |
| Spark | Randomly moving projectiles bounce off terrain until they expire. Duration, speed and pierce increase chances to revisit targets, with a per-cast hit interval. | Spark + Pierce + Spell Echo; projectile speed/duration. Inquisitor is one crit route; Call of the Brotherhood is a lightning-to-cold conversion route. [Spark](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Spark), [historical 3.20 example](https://www.poe-vault.com/guides/spark-inquisitor-build-guide?comment=comment-38698). | Appam skitters along the street. Seed movement and suppress repeated collision hits. Our open street needs explicit boundaries; unseen walls cannot decide damage. |
| Volatile Dead | Consume corpses; each creates a local corpse explosion and a homing fire orb. Corpse-life damage and orb spell damage scale separately. Orbs are **not projectiles**. | Desecrate supplies corpses; Volatile Dead consumes them; Spell Cascade can repeat the targeted area. Necromancer or Elementalist can support the engine. [VD](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Volatile_Dead), [Spellslinger example](https://mobalytics.gg/poe/builds/volatile-dead-spellslinger-elementalist-starter?_lang=en_us). | Use glowing crumbs, never corpses. Supply and consumption are two jobs; boss fights need a non-kill supply. Projectile bonuses must not accidentally scale homing helpers. |
| Spellslinger | A non-triggered wand attack triggers linked spells. Wand damage contributes; linked skills reserve mana, still have costs and share trigger cooldown rules. | Wand attack + Spellslinger + chosen spell; Desecrate then VD is one engine. Reservation efficiency, resource recovery and cooldown recovery must fit. [Spellslinger](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Spellslinger). | A regular idli volley can launch one loaded murtabak. Automation costs a slot/rate; faster throwing cannot bypass the trigger cooldown. Define producer-before-consumer order. |
| Cast on Crit / item triggers | An attack's crit triggers a linked spell; reliable hit and crit chance, attack rhythm, spell cost and cooldown all matter. Other triggers use other conditions. | Attack + Cast On Critical Strike + spell; Cyclone is a mobile hit source. Cospri's Malice separately triggers socketed cold spells on melee crits. [CoC](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Cast_On_Critical_Strike_Support), [Cospri](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Cospri%27s_Malice). | Crit changes what fires, not just the number. Track trigger origin; child effects cannot trigger their parent. Never reproduce exploit-dependent chains. |
| Cyclone | Move while channelling rapid melee hits in a local area. Scale attack speed, area and weapon damage, or use the hits to drive triggers. | Cyclone + melee/area supports for direct damage; Cyclone + CoC + spell for a different engine. Slayer is a direct-damage route. [Cyclone](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Cyclone). | Vadai sweeps the counter while Kannan slides. Short reach is the cost; he still cannot walk up the street. Movement is a build choice, not a universal upgrade. |
| Tornado Shot / hurricane shape | A primary shot creates a secondary fan at a target point. Projectile count creates more fans; speed extends secondary reach. Hits from the same fan are constrained. | Tornado Shot + multiple-projectile / chain choices; Deadeye specialises in projectiles. Dying Sun supplies extra projectiles during flask uptime in historical builds. [Tornado Shot](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Tornado_Shot), [3.19 guide](https://www.poe-vault.com/guides/tornado-shot-deadeye-build-guide). | Telur bursts or Thosai Typhoon give wide coverage. “Hurricane” here means the owner's swirling area fantasy, not a PoE skill name. Cap fragments and per-volley boss hits. |
| Minions | Summoned actors attack independently. Scale their damage, lifetime/count and attack rules; player attack bonuses do not automatically apply. | Summon Raging Spirit + Minion Damage / Unleash; Necromancer. Triad Grip converts minion damage; Tavukai drains spirit life for an alternate engine. [SRS and uniques](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Summon_Raging_Spirit). | Steamed idli helpers keep serving while Kannan dodges. A count cap and short lifetime replace a pet army. State exactly which dips they inherit. |
| Totems | A stationary proxy uses a supported skill. Placement, proxy count and cast speed are separate from the player's own casts. | Spell Totem or Ballista Totem; Hierophant's Pursuit of Faith / Ritual of Awakening; Ancestral Bond adds a totem but forbids personal skill damage. [Totems](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Totem), [Bond](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Ancestral_Bond). | A little service tray auto-serves a linked bread. Counter position matters; the price is weaker direct throws or an occupied bread slot. |
| Traps / mines | Traps fire when an enemy trips them; mines are detonated in a sequence. Placement, arming, charges and detonation form a preparation cycle. | Trap Support or Blastchain/High-Impact Mine Support; Saboteur is a specialisation route. Mines reserve a resource while laid. [Traps](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Trap), [Mines](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Mine). | Appam bowls wait in lanes; stopping can detonate a batch. Clear floor shapes matter more than particles. Add one delivery mode at a time, not three new buttons. |
| Pathfinder / Mageblood | Pathfinder improves flask supply, effect and recovery. Mageblood makes selected magic utility effects permanent, bypassing use/charge expenditure for those effects. | Pathfinder + life/utility flasks; Master Surgeon sustains life-flask effects with reduced recovery. Mageblood builds roll strong utility flasks; life and unique flasks are not made permanent by it. [Pathfinder](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Pathfinder), [Mageblood](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Mageblood). | Drinks can drive a build. Permanent utility is a distinct mode: no on-sip triggers and no permanent healing. Keep burst and sustain builds competitive. |
| Headhunter | Rare kills lend monster modifiers temporarily; effectiveness depends on the encounters available. It is not unconditional boss power. | Headhunter + rare-dense content; borrowed buffs amplify a viable base build. [Headhunter](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Headhunter). | An Inspector's headband borrows one safe pest trait. Cap active traits; sparse bosses expose its weakness. Never borrow enemy invulnerability or teleportation. |
| Elementalist heralds | Reservation skills add damage and conditional secondary effects. Elementalist can improve herald reservation/buff effect, ailments and exposure. Different heralds have different trigger rules. | Herald of Ash / Ice / Thunder; Bringer of Ruin improves herald investment; Mastermind of Discord strengthens exposure. [Elementalist](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Elementalist). | A condiment tray can add several small follow-ups, at the cost of fewer links. Do not repeat old “triple-herald” or aura-stacker interactions as current rules. |

## Enablers and drawbacks

| Pattern | Reference mechanism | Design lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion | Call of the Brotherhood changes Spark's damage route; Triad Grip changes minions. | Convert once before scaling. A spice-to-heat recipe opens a new tree branch; never count both full damage types. |
| Replacement | Totem, trap or trigger supports change who uses a skill or when. | Print “instead” clearly. A slot cannot secretly retain full manual damage after gaining automated damage. |
| Separate scaling axes | Toxic Rain pods, poison, VD corpse blast and spell orb differ. | Tags must distinguish hit, DoT, area, projectile and helper. Describe the affected part of a dish. |
| Coverage versus focus | Concentrated Effect; extra fans and pods cover more space. | Wide clear needs a boss cost. Let players deliberately choose a narrow, loaded version. |
| Resource becomes offence | RF sustain; flask charges; Spellslinger reservation. | Defence, serving rhythm and drink supply can be build stats. Show what runs out. |
| Threshold engine | CoC crit reliability plus cooldown; poison ramp. | Show “ready” or progress pips. Avoid invisible timing breakpoints that punish a tiny upgrade. |
| Trade a whole axis | [Resolute Technique](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Resolute_Technique) prevents evasion but removes crits. | A consistent-throw keystone can sacrifice crit. RT does **not** bypass block; our shield bypass would be an explicit different rule. |
| Change the health model | [Chaos Inoculation](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Chaos_Inoculation) leaves one life and chaos immunity; energy shield becomes the main defence. | Do not copy it as “one hit kills.” A stall ward must exist before offering the equivalent; healing and recharge need explicit rules. |
| Crit as uptime | [Elemental Overload](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/List_of_critical_strike_passive_skills) rewards a skill's recent crit with elemental amplification, removing extra crit damage. | Crit chance can maintain seasoning instead of burst. A no-crit keystone must be incompatible with this route. |
| Borrowed power | Headhunter needs rare kills; Berek spread needs kills and neighbours. | Encounter-dependent power needs a fallback on bosses. No infinite chain damage or infinitely refreshed copied durations. |
| Permanent utility | Mageblood does not count as flask use. | Continuous drinks must not also generate endless on-sip attacks or charge refunds. |

Recommended order: one scaling family → a linked support → a rule-changing item → a sustain/trigger route → an optional combined route. The rule change should be visible within the next wave.

## Borderlands 2: loot that changes how you play

BL2 characters have three skill trees. Deep nodes and capstones give each tree a destination; spending first in one tree, then crossing into another, produces staged build identities. Maya's Motion, Harmony and Cataclysm are an example. [Skill catalogue](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Skill), [Maya trees](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Maya/Skill_tree).
Red text signals a special behaviour, including on blue uniques; “legendary” is not the only source of identity. Sand Hawk fires a moving bird-shaped spread; Grog Nozzle combines slag and healing with weak gun damage. [Special weapon effects](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Weapon_Effects_%28Borderlands_2%29), [Sand Hawk](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Sand_Hawk).
Class mods bridge loot and the tree. They boost specific skill ranks, including beyond the normal allocation cap; in BL2 the skill needs an invested point. Relics focus a second axis: Bone of the Ancients pairs an elemental bonus with action-skill cooldown. [Class mods](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Class_Mod_%28Borderlands_2%29), [Bone](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Bone_of_the_Ancients).

| Named engine | Gear + tree interaction | What feels good / what to borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Krieg Bloodsplosion | Bloodlust builds stacks; the Bloodsplosion capstone makes kills erupt in the killing element and adds overkill. Novas can kill into further novas. Fastball or a strong finishing hit starts the chain. [Mechanics](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodsplosion), [public boss guide](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Voracidous_the_Invincible_%28mission%29). | Prepare a crowd, then pop it. Borrow the setup and sequence; bound overkill, generation depth and boss contribution. |
| Maya Cloud Kill / Cataclysm | Shooting creates one lingering corrosive cloud. Converge groups targets; Ruin adds several elemental debuffs to Phaselock. An SMG class mod and elemental/cooldown relic focus the loop. [Tree](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Maya/Skill_tree), [relic](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Bone_of_the_Ancients). | A cheap shot makes a large environmental effect. Cloud Kill is a mid-tree gamechanger, **not** a capstone; unlock useful identity early. |
| Salvador Gunzerker / Money Shot | Dual wielding lets complementary weapons operate together. Money Shot rewards the last magazine shot; Inconceivable can avoid consumption and repeat it. Healing/slag in one hand complements damage in the other. [Money Shot](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Money_Shot), [Gunzerking interactions](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Fight_For_Your_Life), [Grog behaviour](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Weapon_Effects_%28Borderlands_2%29). | The final idli in a volley can launch a loaded bread. Use a declared deterministic volley rule; do not copy hand-specific exploits. |

Elements create reasons to change gear: fire against flesh, shock against shields, corrosion against armour. Slag primes targets for other damage; its stronger UVHM form pairs with much tougher enemies. Our lesson: optional spice/tender setup, never a mandatory debuff tax. Wiki pages disagree on slag duration, so none is copied. [Elements](https://borderlands.fandom.com/fr/wiki/Slag), [UVHM](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Vault_Hunter_Mode_%28Borderlands_2_and_Borderlands%3A_The_Pre-Sequel%29).
Badass Rank makes broad challenge completion benefit all present and future characters, with diminishing returns and a toggle. Borrow a capped recipe-mastery track, not endless global stats. Ranked specials should use standardised loadouts. [Badass Rank](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Badass_Rank).
OP levels extend the challenge after the character cap by raising enemy/loot levels. They create a next rung but can require replacing a working kit. Borrow selectable Second Shifts with new patterns and sidegrades; never copy paid level-cap access. Exact OP limits depend on edition/DLC; the cited guide describes the older OP8 era. [Digistruct Peak guide](https://www.gamerguides.com/borderlands-2/guide/other-dlc/digistruct-peak-challenge/introduction).

## MapleStory: a next step at several distances

This section adds account collection and destination pacing to the first teardown's jobs, matrices and gear. GMS/SEA/KMS rules differ; no regional counts or level gates become Prata Raja values.

| System | Public rule | Lesson / trap |
|---|---|---|
| Link skills | Characters lend a class-specific skill to another character in the same account/world, within a limited equipped collection. [Link skills](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Link_Skill). | Trying another archetype can help an old favourite. Earn one small recipe stamp per food family; do not require levelling an army of alts. |
| Legion | Character level/job grants member effects; arranging members on a grid adds account-wide bonuses. [Legion](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Legion_System). | Collection can be a loadout puzzle rather than another damage ladder. A small menu-board arrangement could be later sidegrades; defer a full grid. |
| Symbols and daily cadence | Arcane Symbols supply area power and stats; additional symbols come from regional daily quests/activities. Sacred Symbols serve later areas. [Symbols](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Arcane_Symbols). | One clear “closer to the next place” meter. Replace mandatory daily accumulation with materials from any qualifying run and banked optional chores. |
| Boss ladder | The Guide orders bosses by difficulty and rewards; a planner tracks daily/weekly/monthly clears. [Bosses](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Bosses). | Show the next boss, its readable test and one relevant drop. Unlimited practice; progression cannot depend on a missed weekly slot. |
| Overlapping goals | Job identity, account collection, region growth and boss gear run in parallel. This is our inference from the systems above. | A failed run still moves a recipe; a finished recipe changes a run; a boss attempt opens a place. Surface one primary next step, with two optional alternatives. |

Our progression distances: **next run** = try one pairing; **next few runs** = earn a unique recipe; **next chapter** = learn a boss and job rule; **late game** = refine a build or raise a chosen difficulty. These should coexist without daily chores becoming admission tickets.

## Useful checks from other games

- Last Epoch specialises individual skills and combines unique behaviour with crafted affixes. Forging Potential limits further crafting; Legendary Potential permits affix transfer. Transfer has randomness, so it is not a guaranteed perfect item. Borrow a small visible craft budget with guaranteed selected changes; no item destruction. [Beginner guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/last-epoch/beginners-guide-to-last-epoch), [crafting guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/last-epoch/crafting-guide).
- Diablo 3 sets give a clear shopping goal but some builds only come alive at the final piece. Its Legacy of Dreams route supports mixed legendaries without active set bonuses. Borrow useful two-piece identity plus optional three-piece behaviour; retain competitive mixed gear. [DMO starter](https://www.icy-veins.com/d3/wizard-delseres-magnum-opus-dmo-fresh-70-starter-build), [LoD gear guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/d3/lod-frozen-orb-wizard-bis-gear-gems-paragon-points).
- VS evolutions make a known pair produce a named payoff. Brotato's weapon classes reward counted commitment, including drawbacks; it does not share VS's universal weapon-plus-passive evolution model. These are already covered in [VS/Brotato](teardown-vs-brotato-oss.md). Keep S3's recipes, then let linked dips change the route to them.
