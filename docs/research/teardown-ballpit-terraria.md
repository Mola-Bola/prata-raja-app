### A2. Fusion and evolution
Fusion Reactor: an item enemies drop mid-run, not bought. Its menu:

| Option | Needs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Fission (always) | any balls or passives | random upgrade levels; all at L3 gives gold |
| Fusion | 2 unfused L3 balls | one combined ball; frees a slot |
| Evolution | listed L3 pieces (2; Nosferatu needs 3+) | you pick one evolved ball or passive; frees a slot |

- A pair that can evolve must evolve, not fuse. Evolved balls can fuse or evolve again; fused balls cannot. Fused results are player-made (wiki: about 7,900 variants).
- Evolutions (guides): Bleed + Vampire = Vampire Lord; Bleed + Charm = Berserk; Brood Mother + Cell = Maggot; Brood Mother + Vampire = Mosquito King.

### A3. Passives (wiki: 54 base, 17 evolved)
Base passives are on by default or unlock by clearing a layer. Evolved ones mostly take two base passives: Wings of the Anointed = Radiant Feather + Fleet Feet (balls +40% speed, you +20%, immune to ground hazards); Deadeye's Cross = four hilted daggers (60% crit); Soul Reaver = Vampiric Sword + Everflowing Goblet (+1 HP per kill).

### A5. Town, harvest, resources
New Ballbylon: 70+ buildings, each built from a blueprint (9-12 per layer) that enemies and bosses drop. Harvest: characters bounce off tiles, 1 unit per hit; one free harvest per trip; Gold Mine pays 1-2 gold per bounce (fan guides).

| Group | Examples (wiki) | What it feeds the next dive |
|---|---|---|
| Harvest | Farm, Lumberyard, Stone Mine (auto with an assigned character); Worker's Guild (offline 24 h, reduced); Market (gold to goods); Spa (gold to re-harvest now) | wheat, wood, stone, gold |
| Run-start | Gemsmith (pick an extra ball), Antique Shop (pick a passive), Jeweler (starter ball level), Bag Maker (+1 ball slot), Carpenter (+1 passive slot) | starting kit |
| In-run | Gambler's Den (free rerolls), Meditation Tent (4 level-up choices), Abbey (XP), Bank (gold), Necromancer (one revive per battle), Matchmaker (two characters) | run rules |
| Stat towers | 6 capped (+1 per level, max +5); 6 uncapped, cost climbs fast (Hospital rank 27 to 28 = 199,920 wheat); layer trophies per first clear | permanent stats; a sink |

### A6. Pacing and why it hooks
- A dive is about 15 minutes: a vertical descent that ends in death or the boss (review). Our runs take about 6 min (SPEC), so our pull has to come from the stall loop. Eight layers; each unlock needs gears from first clears by any characters (2, 2, 2, 3, 4, 4, 5 for layers 2-8); New Game+ asks 8 per layer (wiki).
- Loop: dive, harvest, build, dive. First clear takes 12-15 h; New Game+ about double (review).
- Hooks (reviews): losing still leaves gains, so restarts feel quick; the town needs resources, so another dive is the next harvest; fusion and evolution are the joy; gems from each stage add chances at new balls.
- Complaints: easy until New Game+, repetition late, the town wears thin after several runs (Checkpoint, Game Informer).
- Sun, per news pieces: the prototype was turn-based until Vampire Survivors pushed it real-time; evolution combos drew on Doodle God guides; the base stays tied to the core action, not a plain menu.

### B2. Gear tiers and the crafting chain
Ore, bar, gear. Furnace (Work Bench recipe: 20 stone block, 4 wood, 3 torch) smelts pre-Hardmode ores: 3 ore per bar for Copper, Tin, Iron, Lead, Cobalt, Demonite, Crimtane, Palladium; 4 per bar for Silver, Gold, Tungsten, Platinum, Mythril, Orichalcum. Hellforge smelts Hellstone. Anvils turn bars into gear: Iron/Lead, then Mythril/Orichalcum (same recipes plus new ones).

| Tier | Gate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Copper/Tin, Iron/Lead, Silver/Tungsten, Gold/Platinum | underground ore | defense sets +2 to +4 |
| Demonite / Crimtane | Eater of Worlds / Brain of Cthulhu drop the big ore | pre-Hardmode alternate chain |
| Cobalt/Palladium, Mythril/Orichalcum, Adamantite/Titanium | Hardmode ore, gated by pickaxe tier | page ties them to no single boss |
| Hallowed, then Chlorophyte | Hallowed bars drop only from the 3 mechanical bosses; Chlorophyte ore (Underground Jungle) needs a Drax or Pickaxe Axe, which take Hallowed Bars and a Soul per mechanical | needs all 3 mechanicals |

Stations (crafting page): Work Bench, Furnace, Hellforge, Iron/Lead and Mythril/Orichalcum Anvils, Loom, Sawmill, Tinkerer's Workshop, Alchemy Table, Crystal Ball, Imbuing Station, Cooking Pot, Lihzahrd Furnace.

### B4. Accessories and Tinkerer combos
- Duplicate accessories cannot be equipped; item modifiers stack freely. Slots: 5 in Classic, up to 7 in Master with a Demon Heart.
- Tinkerer's Workshop (needs research): two accessories become one that uses one slot; inputs' modifiers are lost and the result rolls a new one. The page does not say whether combined effects stack with other accessories.

| Inputs | Result |
|---|---|
| Obsidian Shield + Ankh Charm | Ankh Shield |
| Celestial Stone + Moon Shell | Celestial Shell |
| Rocket Boots + Hermes Boots | Spectre Boots (rocket jumps, fast movement) |
| Gold Ring + Lucky Coin | Coin Ring |

### B5. Reforging (prefixes)
Goblin Tinkerer rerolls one random modifier for coins: equal odds, never leaves an item unmodified. Cost is about a third of item value, so bad rolls are cheaper; Discount Card or Greedy Ring take 20% off.

| Prefix | Fits | Adds | Item value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legendary | melee swings, whips | +15% dmg, +10% speed, +5% crit, +10% size, +15% KB | +210% |
| Godly | any weapon | +15% dmg, +5% crit, +15% KB | +112% |
| Demonic | weapons without knockback | +15% dmg, +5% crit | +60% |
| Menacing | accessory | +4% damage | +44% |
| Warding | accessory | +4 defense; often rated the best accessory prefix | +44% |
| Broken | weapon (bad) | -30% dmg, -20% KB | -69% |
