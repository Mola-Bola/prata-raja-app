Almost none of these guides describe telegraphs or phase HP.
- What makes a pattern dodgeable:
  - A cue that ends in one lane is fair when it leaves time to move: a line that vanishes just before the shot, or a charge start.
  - Countable beats (three hops then a leap; two volleys then a charge) let players plan.
  - Built-in gaps (five-way spread, safe tiles, bubble gaps) turn an attack into a read.
  - Fixed windows (Bat's gap) reward a shot, which is the stop-to-shoot trade.
  - Unfair class: unsignalled attacks (shard rain, Tree's random jumps, Ice Mage shards, Tentacle spawn on your spot). Keep these rare.
  - Prata's dodge is x-only, so diagonal and radial patterns do not port. Use lanes, gap walls and horizontal lines (game-feel section 6).

## 6. Port to Prata Raja

Rules for the port:
- Keep the one-thumb, stop-to-throw loop. Kannan's dodge is x-only, so telegraphs are lanes and lines.
- Keep energy, ads and gem-gated chests out ([engagement.md](/Users/molabola/prata-raja/docs/research/engagement.md)). Pity stays at 5 and 20 unless testers ask.
- One slot change only: a 6th pet slot (idea 15). Everything else fits the five gear slots.
- Enemy gaps vs Archero: bouncing shooter (Meatball, Snake) for ch2 hawker centre; beam lane (Inferno Tower) for ch3 Monsoon Alley; floor mines (Ghost Bomber) for ch4 Durian Row.
