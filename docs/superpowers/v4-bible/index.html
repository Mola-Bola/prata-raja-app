Prata Raja: Wood Legend

v4 direction. Everything is a wood and paper cut-out. A hawker's daydream that reaches back along the old monsoon and spice routes: Kannan takes on the mantles of historic heroes and mythic archetypes as he ascends. Heroes and creatures, never gods. v2 and v3 stay as references.

Material: wood

The same Kannan in six wood treatments. Pick the one the whole game is built from.

Legendary parallax

Like the starfield inside a Guild Wars legendary, but wood: a window of deep layered grain inside Kannan's armour that shifts as he moves. Each ascension unlocks a deeper grain. In the game this is a shader: grain layers masked by the sprite, scrolling at different rates.

Routes

Working names, from the owner's naming direction: a hawker word plus a rank from heraldry, voyages or soldiering. Tiers unlock through milestones (bosses, chapters, discovered combos), not a fixed level ladder.

Ascendancies

Two per route, unlocked by bosses and story. They change how the route plays, not just its numbers.

Bosses

Each blends a Singapore hawker place with a mythic creature, and guards a milestone.

Drain Serpent

A world-serpent coiled through the monsoon storm drains of a shophouse street.

Queue Hydra

Pest heads rising from a tangle of hawker trays and queue poles.

Wet Market Minotaur

Guardian of a labyrinth of fish crates and ice at 4 a.m.

Grease Kraken

Tentacles of hot oil and charred wood rising from a giant wok.

Rat King

A crown of intertwined tails on a throne of mahjong tiles and stacked stools.

Moth Queen

Circling the sodium lamp, wings patterned like faded shophouse tiles.

Living cut-outs

Static scenery, one-piece sway (lamps, umbrellas, stools), and puppets with jointed parts for anything you interact with or that matters to the story: Kannan, the kopi uncle, carts, bosses. The ice kacang cart's crank turns, the ice shaves, the syrup bottles tilt.
