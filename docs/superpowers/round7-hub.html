ROUND 7 · HUB REIMAGINED

The stall is a place you walk into.

Four fresh ways to build Prata Raja's home between runs, each in a real phone frame. Direction 1, the workbench in 3 and the day shift in 4 are playable: walk Kannan with the joystick, WASD or a tap, drag dips into katori, serve regulars. A shared run HUD closes the loop so hub and street feel like one game.

DIRECTION 1 · PLAYABLE

Walkable Stall

Soul Knight's living room, but it smells of ghee. Kannan walks a three-quarter stall inside the hawker centre and every station is a prop within three seconds of him.

Playable: drag the joystick, tap the floor, or WASD / arrows. Walk up to a prop, tap it or press E.
Station frame: Gear Rack × Forge, one bench for wear, stash and enhance. Two levels deep, no more.
Codex concept render: the target look for this direction.
What players loveHades House and Soul Knight's Living Room make the hub a place with people and props in it. Stations you can see are easier to remember than menu tabs, and the room can visibly grow.
RiskDave the Diver's walking slog and Diablo 4's distant stash and smith. Answers here: every station within ~3 s of Kannan, one persistent GO pill, a panel that closes when you walk off, and Gear + Forge + Stash fused into one bench.
SensitivitiesThe kolam is a framed board on the wall, never a floor event. No deities, no shrine prop.
Build costMedium. One tile room, about ten sprites, a tiny collision list. Panels reuse the existing build sheet data.
DIRECTION 2

Hawker Centre Village

Your stall is one of nine. The neighbours are the stations, and the whole centre lights up, stall by stall, as the story moves.

Scroll sideways or tap a stall: Kannan walks there. Lit stalls are unlocked, dark ones are the next story beats.
Base growth: the same spot at three story milestones.
Codex concept render: the target look for this direction.
What players lovePeople. Hades' cast, gifts and gossip give every return a reason to walk somewhere. Soul Knight's renovation and Halls of Torment's camp show progress as a place. Regulars you know by name make the hub remember you.
RiskCult of the Lamb: bases that grow too fast and nag. Keep the village to nine stalls, no upkeep timers. It is wider than one screen, so walking time matters: tap-to-walk and a pinned GO.
Build costHighest art cost: each NPC wants a portrait and lines. Cheap start with three stalls and a lit, dark, dark pattern.
DIRECTION 3 · PLAYABLE

The Workbench

One steel counter, no walking. Everything is a thing you drag onto another thing, and the counter sizzles when you get it right.

Playable drags: gear onto the tawa, dips into the katori or the pot, the roti into the tiffin.
The canteen moment: cook one pre-run meal from the spice rack. Its buff rides on the GO pill.
Codex concept render: the target look for this direction.
What players lovePotion Craft makes crafting the room itself. Monster Hunter's canteen is the ritual players actually remember: eat once, hunt, the buff expires on return.
RiskDrag-and-drop on a phone needs generous drop targets and an undo. Without a room there is no social layer, so pair it with D1 or D2 for people.
Build costLowest art cost (all props already exist). Medium UI cost for the drag layer. Fits the existing gear, dip socket and enhance data as is.
DIRECTION 4

Day & Night

Dave the Diver for roti. Night is the run. Day is sixty seconds of tap-serving the regulars from what you hauled home.

Playable day shift: tap the bread that matches the speech bubble. Ends after 60 s.
Dusk transition: day tally, shutters down, tonight's run.
Codex concept render: the target look for this direction.
What players loveTwo loops that feed each other, and the restaurant as a comforting job. Each step pays out. Regulars give bonds and a reason to care about quality.
RiskThe Dave GDC talk admits the split can feel monotonous, and players call the restaurant walking and action screens a slog. Keep it under 2 minutes, tap-only, skippable, and never the only source of power.
Build costMedium. New loop, new balance (coins, rep, bonds). Highest design risk because it adds a second game to tune. Do it last.
SHARED

Run HUD for freeform movement

The stall joystick is the run joystick. Kannan keeps the same sprite, scale and thumb position, so crossing the door changes the scenery and nothing else.

Auto-throw toward the nearest foe. The top bar is grouped into three clusters. The hub meal buff stays visible all run.
Layout rulesLeft thumb: joystick, same spot as the hub. Right thumb: dodge and dip socket readouts only. Top: level ring and HP left, stop and timer centre, coins and pause right. One thin XP bar under it.
Hub linkThe buff chip from the Kopi uncle or tiffin meal shows for the whole run and clears on return, as in Monster Hunter. Pests are real enemy art.

Recommended fusion

Ship D1's room with D3's bench inside it. Grow it with D2 over time. Keep D4 as an optional later chapter.

  1. Now: D1 walkable stall, with Gear Rack × Forge built as the D3 workbench (drag gear to the tawa, dips into katori). One station, one trip.
  2. Next: Kopi uncle and the tiffin meal as the pre-run buff, shown on GO and in the run HUD.
  3. Then: D2 growth. Each story beat lights a neighbouring stall and adds one stool, sign or stove to the room. Regulars give gifts and bonds.
  4. Later: D4 day shift as an opt-in sixty-second screen that turns spare materials into coins and bonds.
