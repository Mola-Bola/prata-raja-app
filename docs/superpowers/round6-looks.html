What goes where. Tawa: rotis and dips. Bowls: dip collection. Rack: gear and forge. Kannan: spec tree. Window: map of places. Menu board: Chef’s Special. Plus calendar, tip jar, training board, chest.

What it removes. Every text pill. Labels show only on tap or hover; badges stay for anything ready. Quiet glow pulses so new players find the spots.

Dynamic. Glow rings, steam, lamp, jar glint, a tray that explains the selected spot, an order chit in the run.

Quick comparison

  • A reads cleaner and is cheaper to build (one dock plus the sheets we already have). Weak spot: it is a normal app menu, so the art is only a backdrop.
  • B is more charming and in-theme, and fits an ARPG home base. Weak spot: discoverability, tap accuracy on small objects, and each new station needs art space.
  • They mix: B’s glow-and-label hotspots on A’s dock, if the owner likes both.
