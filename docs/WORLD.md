# Prata Raja — world and progression (design direction, 12 Oct)

Owner ask: progression, gear and enemies should feel like one hawker world, "not plunked or pasted on top of each other". Rule: every number you earn shows up as a thing you can see, in the stall or on Kannan.

## The stall is the progress bar
- Stall level changes the hub art in tiers (rendered variants, not overlays): **L1** a humble stall · **L3** a proper signboard and a second stool · **L5** fairy lights and a queue of regulars · **L7** a framed newspaper clipping about the stall · **L10** "Raja's Stall": golden tawa, the regulars cheering.
- Coins drop into the tip jar on the counter (it visibly fills); boss trophies stand on a shelf; the calendar on the pillar tears off a page per streak day.
- Recipes are cooked on the hub tawa (tap the tawa → short cooking moment → the new bread joins the stack); unlocked dips become bowls on the hub counter — the same bowls you see on the counter in a run.

## Gear is worn, not listed
- Towel, headband, apron, gloves and slippers show on Kannan in the hub **and** in runs: each worn piece is a separate cut-out layer on his poses, coloured by the item and glowing by rarity (Rare blue, Epic purple, Legendary gold, Raja shimmer).
- In the daydream his gear becomes the hero version (towel → cape, headband → crown); the crown's metal tracks stall level (bronze → silver → gold), so progress shows mid-fight.

## The daydream map
- Chapters are places on a hand-drawn paper map pinned to the stall's menu board: 1 Kopitiam Street → 2 Hawker Centre at Night → 3 Pasar Malam → 4 Wet Market → 5 Food Street finale. Each place brings its own pests and one boss.
- Chef's Special (daily) is the dish of the day chalked on the same board.

## Pests that belong where they appear
- Ch1 street: ants, roaches, pigeons, **mynahs** (swoop to the counter and steal a prata: one bread goes quiet for a few seconds unless you hit the mynah first), **drain rats** (pop out of the longkang grates mid-street).
- Ch2 hawker centre: tray rats, chope tissue packets, neon moths, and mynah flocks around the tables.
- Later: a comedic **Grade Inspector** boss (a clipboard-and-grade-sign spirit, not a real officer): marks lanes for "hygiene checks", "grades down" your dips for a few seconds, fires paper fine notices.
- Spawns enter from the far end of each place, out of drains or from behind stalls in the art; they never pop out of walls.

## Powers in theme (to design with Codex)
Each dip's power comes from what the food does: condensed milk = sugar crash (keep), sambal = tears (blinded pests wander), masala = sneeze cloud (lingering spreads), mint chutney = cooling breeze (pushback + slow), teh tarik = the pull (a long poured stream), dhal = comfort (heal on kill). Card borders follow the same rule: banana leaf, steel thali, brass kolam, rangoli.

## Story (motion comic, owner ask 12 Oct)
Rendered story panels with slow pans/zooms, short Singlish captions, sound, tap to advance, Skip always visible. Plays once (replayable from the hub's menu board); flags in the save.
- **Prologue (first launch, before run 1):** 5:30 a.m. Kopitiam Street, Kannan opens the stall → breakfast rush ("Two kosong! One tissue!") → the lull: a prata spinning on his fingertip starts to glow → golden swirl, he becomes Prata Raja (towel → cape, headband → crown) → pests pour out of the drains, King Cockroach on a rooftop → over the shoulder at the counter: "Not my stall." → run 1.
- **Chapter beats:** each boss win ends with a wake-up gag (an uncle shaking him awake) and a teaser of the next place (chapter 2: the night shift at the hawker centre, a burger-shaped shadow under the neon). Each new chapter opens with 2–3 panels as he dozes off again somewhere new.
